Request Account Deletion

Lernivo users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to delete your account:

  1. Open the Lernivo application.
  2. Go to Account Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the deletion notice and confirm your request.

If you cannot access the application, contact our support team using the contact details below to request account deletion.
