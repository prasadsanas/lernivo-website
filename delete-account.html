Delete Your Lernivo Account

You have the right to request deletion of your Lernivo account and associated personal data.

Account deletion is permanent once completed. Your account and associated information may no longer be recoverable.

Option 1: Request deletion through the app

  1. Open the Lernivo application.
  2. Navigate to Account Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the deletion notice.
  5. Confirm your request.

Option 2: Request deletion without accessing the app

If you cannot access the application, send an account deletion request to our support email.

Include the email address associated with your Lernivo account.

We may ask for reasonable verification to confirm that you own the account.

Never send your password or OTP.

Email Account Deletion Request

What information is involved?

Your account may contain your name, email address and optional WhatsApp number. Study information may also be stored locally on your device.

Our deletion process is intended to permanently delete your Appwrite account and associated cloud profile data. Local study information and backups on your device or other storage locations may need to be deleted separately.

Deletion processing and data retention

We will verify your request and communicate the expected completion time. Any information retained for legal obligations will be explained to you, including the reason and applicable retention period.

We will confirm when the deletion request has been completed.
