Delete Your Lernivo Account

You can request deletion of your Lernivo account and associated personal data. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: Delete from the app

  2. Navigate to Account Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the deletion information.
  5. Confirm your deletion request.
