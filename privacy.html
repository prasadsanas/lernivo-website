Privacy Policy

App: Lernivo — Study Planner

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lernivo collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our Android study planning application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Lernivo may process:

  • Account information: Name and email address used for registration and authentication.
  • WhatsApp number: An optional phone number that you may provide in your profile. It is not considered verified unless a separate verification process is completed.
  • Study information: Subjects, tasks, chapters, examinations, study schedules and focus sessions that you create in the application.
  • App preferences: Settings and locally stored information needed to maintain application behavior.
  • Backup information: Data included in a JSON backup when you choose to export your study data.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate you and support email verification and password recovery.
  • Maintain your profile information.
  • Save and organize your study plans and progress.
  • Provide study timers, reminders and progress summaries.
  • Support data backup and restoration when requested.
  • Respond to support and account deletion requests.

3. Where Your Information Is Stored

Appwrite Cloud: Lernivo uses Appwrite Cloud for account authentication and cloud profile information.

Local device storage: Study information is stored locally using the Room database. Application preferences and certain session or timer information may be stored using Android local storage mechanisms.

Backups: If you export a backup, the resulting file is saved to a location you select. Its subsequent storage and security depend on that location and your actions.

4. Notifications and Background Activity

Lernivo may use Android notification and background-work facilities to provide study reminders and related features. Notification permission is requested where required by Android. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

5. Sharing of Information

Account and profile information is processed through Appwrite Cloud as part of providing the service.

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers involved in operating the application, or disclosed where required by law.

6. Data Retention

Account information is retained while your account remains active and as necessary to provide the service.

Local study information may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the application data or uninstall the app. Exported backup files may remain wherever you saved them until you delete those files.

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable law. Any such retention will be limited to the relevant purpose and period.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Lernivo account and associated personal information from within the application or through our external account deletion page.

If you cannot access the app, email letsworkwithprasad@gmail.com using your registered account email address.

We may verify your identity before processing a request. Local files and exported backups may need to be removed separately from your device or storage provider.

Visit our Account Deletion page.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Lernivo is a study planning application. Its intended audience, age requirements and any additional safeguards for younger users should be considered in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play requirements.

10. Your Choices

You can:

  • Update profile information through the application.
  • Manage notification permissions through Android settings.
  • Export or restore supported study data backups.
  • Request account deletion.
  • Contact us with privacy questions.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application, services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.
